Our Story

About Us

NexaWeb is a student-built showcase for SE-105L at SSUET, proving that academic web projects can be both technically sound and visually remarkable — powered by Flask.

🌐

Our Mission

We set out to prove that academic web projects don't need to be bland. By combining Flask's routing simplicity with thoughtful visual design, NexaWeb gives each page its own aesthetic personality while sharing a unified brand.

From the dark gold-accented home to this warm editorial about section — every view was crafted with intention.
